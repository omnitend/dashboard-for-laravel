IBM Plex Sans, digits only, for the theme's "DX Figures" face (#215).

These are IBM's official Latin1 split woff2 files, copied unmodified from the
npm package @ibm/plex-sans 1.1.0 (fonts/split/woff2/), the upstream of
https://github.com/IBM/plex. The theme limits them to U+0030-0039 with
unicode-range; the files themselves are whole.

"Plex" is a Reserved Font Name under the SIL Open Font License (OFL.txt,
the package's LICENSE.txt, unmodified). A modified copy could not keep the
name, so these files must never be subset, re-encoded or otherwise changed.
To update them, replace them with newer upstream files and record the new
version and checksums here and in tests/bundle/theme-fonts.test.ts.
